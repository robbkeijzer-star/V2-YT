Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-21

DailyTrack__Robin is a personal, single-user dashboard used to track fitness, health, hydration, and finance data. This policy explains how data from connected third-party services (such as WHOOP) is handled.

What data is collected

When you connect your WHOOP account, this app requests access to WHOOP data (such as recovery, sleep, and strain metrics) via WHOOP's OAuth API.

How data is used and stored

Access and refresh tokens returned by WHOOP are passed directly to your browser and stored in your browser's local storage. They are not stored on any server or database operated by this app. Data fetched from WHOOP's API is displayed only to you, in your own browser session.

Data sharing

No data collected through this app is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party other than WHOOP itself (to retrieve your own data).

Revoking access

You can disconnect this app at any time from your WHOOP account settings, or by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.
